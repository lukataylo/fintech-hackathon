Field Guide No. 02 · The Mentor · 18:30–20:30

Ask,
don’t drive.

Teams get under two hours to build. Good mentors don’t fix the code. They ask the one question that makes a team cut scope, name a real user and protect the demo.

Field Guide No. 02For mentors, organisers and anyone helping on the floorThe Mentor
1

The arc of the night

One question at each stage
18:30

Form teams

For whom, with what problem, showing which one flow?

No sentence, no building. Pair up the solo builders.

18:45

Start building

Could anyone build this in a weekend?

Test originality now, not at 20:00. Scam checkers are the default fraud idea.

19:00

Build check

What works end to end right now?

Then: what is the one blocker, and what can you mock instead?

19:45

Freeze scope

What does the 1:30 story actually need?

No new features, no pivots. Re-run the main flow on fresh data.

20:00

Record

Who narrates, and where is the backup take?

Record by 20:15. Entries close at 20:30 sharp.

2

Where teams get stuck

The same six, at hackathons everywhere
ProblemFix
Five features, none finished→One person, one problem, one flow. Small and working beats big and broken.
No clear user→Name a real person and the money moment where they are stuck.
“Anyone could build this”→Ask what is new. Re-use real rails: Open Banking, bureau data, an existing bank app.
AI code that breaks or leaks→Re-run the main flow on fresh data. Check secrets, inputs and logins.
The demo fails on the night→Mock the slow calls, pre-fill the forms, keep a clean backup recording.
Quiet, or new to all this→Agree roles early. Praise small wins. No question is a dumb one.
3

Do, don’t

Their keyboard is lava

✓Do

  • Ask, listen, let the team choose
  • Say when you’ll be back, and come back
  • Help split roles and pick a narrator
  • Test originality in the first hour
  • One strength before one fix

×Don’t

  • Take the keyboard
  • Pitch your own idea
  • Push a pivot after 19:30
  • Hover once a team says it’s fine
  • Solve it for them
4

The 1:30 demo script

What judges will watch · coach teams to this
0:00Person“Meet [name]. [Their money problem, with a number].”
0:15Why now“Until now, [why it couldn’t be done]. Now [what changed].”
0:25DemoOne flow, live. The wow moment by 1:00. Narrate what we can’t see.
1:05Trust“Real: [this]. Mocked: [that]. We protect users by [how].”
1:20Close“[Who] pays, because [why].” Stop at 1:30.
It feels better to have something small working at the end than something big that’s in shambles.
