2

The scoring rubric

1 to 5 per criterion · weighted to 100
30%

Real-world impact

A named person, a real money problem, a reason it matters now.

A 5: “Traders wait nine days for payouts. This makes it same-day.”
25%

Originality

What is new, in one sentence. Not a chatbot over a statement.

A 5: could not have existed two years ago.
25%

Execution & craft

One flow, start to finish, on screen. Proof beats polish.

A 5: real-looking data, no dead ends.
20%

Trust & responsibility

What could go wrong with money or data, and what they did about it.

A 5: says what is mocked, stored and who could be harmed.

3

Reward, resist

Proof over polish

✓Reward

  • A named person and their problem in the first 20 seconds
  • One complete flow, working on screen
  • Re-use of real rails: Open Banking, bureau data, a bank’s own app
  • Teams that say what is mocked or not built yet
  • Decisions explained in words a customer would understand

×Resist

  • A slick interface with no clear user
  • Four half-built paths instead of one finished one
  • Tours of the tech stack, team CVs and slides
  • Yet another scam checker, unless it shows what is new
  • Marking your first videos harshly: revisit them at the end

Five questions for finalists

Live Q&A · one each, then move on
  1. 1
    Who exactly is this for?One person, not a market segment.
  2. 2
    What did you build tonight, and what did you re-use?
  3. 3
    What breaks first with real customers or real money?
  4. 4
    If this works, what could go right at scale?
  5. 5
    What is mocked, and what would make it real?Ask every team the same set: upside and risk, not one or the other.
